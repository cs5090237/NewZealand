## 📍 PART 1: The Alpine Loop (Christchurch to Queenstown)

### Day 1: Arrival & The Garden City
📅 Sat 13 Dec | 🛌 Stay: Golden Hotel, Riccarton

**10:00 AM:** Arrive Christchurch Airport (CHC). Pick up rental van (Ensure car seat for 2yo is fitted).

**12:00 PM:** Check-in / Bag Drop at Golden Hotel.

**01:00 PM:** Lunch at Dosa Kitchen (Riccarton Road) – South Indian Dosa/Thali (Pure Veg options).

**03:00 PM:** Christchurch Antarctic Centre OR Botanic Gardens.

> 🧒 **Kid Hit:** The Botanic Gardens are free and have a massive playground and pool for the 2yo.

**06:00 PM:** Riverside Market. Walk the Promenade. Dinner options: Herba Gourmet (Salads/Veg) or Castro's (Tapas).

**08:00 PM:** Early night to recover from jetlag.

---

### Day 2: Turquoise Waters
📅 Sun 14 Dec | 🛌 Stay: Hunter 3 Bedroom, Lake Tekapo  
🚗 Drive: 3 hrs (Scenic)

**09:00 AM:** Depart Christchurch.

**11:00 AM:** Stop at Geraldine Farmshop & Cafe. Great cheese/chutney tasting.

**12:30 PM:** Arrive Lake Tekapo. Lunch: Our Dog Friday (Great vegetarian burgers/fries).

**02:00 PM:** Check-in at Hunter Apartment.

**03:30 PM:** Tekapo Springs.

> 🧘 **Relax:** Hot pools facing the lake. There is a specific "Aqua Play" area for toddlers.

**05:30 PM:** Motuariki View Point. Quick stop for panoramic views of Lake Tekapo and the surrounding alps.

**06:00 PM:** Church of the Good Shepherd.

> 📸 **Photo Op:** Golden hour photos. The viewpoint and church are close by, perfect for catching the evening light.

**07:30 PM:** Dinner at Kohinoor Indian Restaurant (Tekapo).

**09:30 PM:** Dark Sky Project Stargazing Experience.

> 🌌 **Must Do:** Lake Tekapo is a UNESCO Dark Sky Reserve. Book the evening stargazing tour to see the Southern Cross, Milky Way, and planets through powerful telescopes. The guides are excellent and it's truly spectacular on a clear night.

---

### Day 3: Glacial Giants & Salmon
📅 Mon 15 Dec | 🛌 Stay: Luxurious Twizel Escape  
🚗 Drive: 45 mins

**09:30 AM:** Mt John Observatory Summit. Drive up ($8 fee) for the famous "Astro Café" view. Hot chocolate for kids.

**11:00 AM:** Drive to Twizel. Stop at NZ Alpine Lavender.

> 📝 **Note:** It's seasonal, but if purple fields are blooming, it's a must-stop. Lavender ice cream!

**12:30 PM:** Stop at High Country Salmon (Floating shop).

> 🎣 **Activity:** Free fish feeding. The kids will go crazy throwing pellets to the giant salmon.

**02:00 PM:** Check-in Twizel.

**04:00 PM:** Lake Ruataniwha. Easy flat walk, great for skipping stones.

**07:00 PM:** Dinner at Fishtail India (Twizel) – Highly rated.

---

### Day 4: Mount Cook Adventure
📅 Tue 16 Dec | 🛌 Stay: Luxurious Twizel Escape  
🚗 Drive: 45 mins each way

**09:00 AM:** Drive to Aoraki/Mount Cook National Park.

**10:00 AM:** Hooker Valley Track (The big one).

> 📋 **Logistics:** You don't need to do the full 3 hours. Walk to the First Swing Bridge (30 mins return). The 11yo will love the bridge; use a carrier for the 2yo.
>
> **Alternative:** Tasman Glacier View Track (Short stairs, 20 mins, view of icebergs).

**01:00 PM:** Picnic Lunch at White Horse Hill Campground.

**03:00 PM:** Sir Edmund Hillary Alpine Centre (Museum/Movie) inside the Hermitage Hotel (Good if weather turns).

**04:30 PM:** Helicopter Glacier Landing Experience (Pre-book from Mount Cook Village).

> 🚁 **Highlight:** Book a scenic helicopter flight with snow landing on the Tasman Glacier. Most operators offer 25-45 minute flights departing from Mount Cook Airport. You'll land on pristine alpine snow with views of Aoraki/Mount Cook. This is weather-dependent, so having it here (rather than West Coast) gives you the best chance of clear skies.

**06:00 PM:** Return to Twizel for rest.

---

## 📍 PART 2: The Adrenaline Capital (Queenstown)

### Day 5: Canyons & Fruit
📅 Wed 17 Dec | 🛌 Stay: Remarkable Views, Queenstown  
🚗 Drive: 2.5 hrs

**09:00 AM:** Depart Twizel.

**09:45 AM:** Clay Cliffs, Omarama.

> 🏜️ **Detour:** 20 mins on gravel road. Alien-like tall rock pinnacles.

**11:00 AM:** Lindis Pass Viewpoint.

**12:30 PM:** Jones Family Fruit Stall (Cromwell).

> 🍦 **Must Eat:** Real Fruit Ice Cream.

**02:30 PM:** Stop at Kawarau Gorge Suspension Bridge (Bungy viewing).

**04:00 PM:** Check-in Remarkable Views.

**05:30 PM:** Jacks Point Playground. One of the best playgrounds in NZ. Flying fox for the 11yo, safe zones for the 2yo.

---

### Day 6: The 8th Wonder (Milford Sound)
📅 Thu 18 Dec | 🛌 Stay: Remarkable Views, Queenstown  
🚁 Pre-Booked: Milford Sound Flight & Cruise (Morning)

**Morning:** Fly-Cruise-Fly.

> ✨ This is the "Mindblowing" part. The flight over the Southern Alps saves you 9 hours of bus travel.

**01:00 PM:** Back in Queenstown.

**01:30 PM:** Lunch at Fergburger (Veggie Bun) or Fergbaker (Pies/Pastries).

**03:00 PM:** Free time to explore Queenstown.

> 🛍️ Options: Stroll the waterfront, browse the shops, or relax at the hotel after your early morning.

**04:00 PM (Optional):** TSS Earnslaw Steamship Cruise.

> ⛴️ **If you have energy:** The vintage 1912 steamship is a relaxing 90-minute cruise across Lake Wakatipu. However, after the Milford Sound cruise this morning, you may prefer to skip this and rest up.

**07:00 PM:** Dinner at Bombay Palace Queenstown.

---

### Day 7: Wanaka & The Tree
📅 Fri 19 Dec | 🛌 Stay: Remarkable Views, Queenstown  
🚗 Drive: 1 hr each way via Crown Range

**09:30 AM:** Drive the steep Crown Range Road. Stop at the Cardrona Bra Fence (Quirky photo op).

**11:00 AM:** Wanaka Lavender Farm. Pet the animals (Alpacas, Sheep).

**12:30 PM:** Lunch near the lake.

**01:30 PM:** #ThatWanakaTree photo stop.

**02:30 PM:** Puzzling World.

> 🧩 **Kids:** Giant Maze and Illusion Rooms. Fun for the whole family.

**05:00 PM:** Stop at Arrowtown Bakery on the way back for a pie/donut. Walk the historic Chinese Settlement in Arrowtown.

---

### Day 8: Adrenaline & Heights
📅 Sat 20 Dec | 🛌 Stay: Remarkable Views, Queenstown

**09:00 AM:** Drive to Glenorchy (45 mins).

> 🏔️ **View:** The "Road to Paradise". Stops at Bennetts Bluff.

**11:00 AM:** Shotover Jet (Arthur's Point).

> ⚡ **Adrenaline:** 360-degree spins. Note: Check min height for 2yo (usually 1m), otherwise swap parents.

**01:00 PM:** Lunch in town.

**04:00 PM:** Skyline Gondola & Luge. Ride the gondola up. Do 3-5 Luge rides. The Blue Track is gentle enough for tandem rides with toddlers.

**Dinner:** Eat at the Stratosfare Restaurant (Buffet with huge veg selection) at the top of the Gondola for sunset views.

---

## 📍 PART 3: The Wild West Coast

### Day 9: Waterfalls & The Pass
📅 Sun 21 Dec | 🛌 Stay: Bella Vista Motel, Fox Glacier  
🚗 Drive: 4.5 hrs (Lots of stops)

**09:00 AM:** Depart Queenstown via Haast Pass.

**11:30 AM:** Blue Pools. Easy walk through forest to crystal clear water.

**01:00 PM:** Fantail Falls & Thunder Creek Falls. Both are 5 min walks from the road.

**02:30 PM:** Lunch at Haast (limited veg options, pack snacks/sandwiches from QT).

**05:00 PM:** Arrive Fox Glacier.

**Dinner:** The White Pub (Pizzas/Wedges) or drive 25 mins to Franz Josef for King Tiger (Asian/Indian).

---

### Day 10: Mirrors & Ice
📅 Mon 22 Dec | 🛌 Stay: Bella Vista Motel, Fox Glacier

**08:30 AM:** Lake Matheson Walk. Mirror reflections of Mt Cook. Pram friendly!

**10:30 AM:** Fox Glacier Valley Walk. View the glacier terminal face from a distance.

**Afternoon:** Relax. Easy afternoon.

---

### Day 11: Turquoise Gorges
📅 Tue 23 Dec | 🛌 Stay: Shining Star Beachfront, Hokitika  
🚗 Drive: 2 hrs

**10:00 AM:** Drive North.

**11:30 AM:** West Coast Treetop Walk. Steel walkway high in the trees. Stroller accessible.

**01:30 PM:** Hokitika Gorge. Vibrant blue water. Must do.

**04:00 PM:** Check-in Shining Star (Right on the beach). Visit the Glowworm Dell (Free, right across the road from your hotel) after dark.

**Dinner:** Priya Indian Restaurant (Hokitika).

---

### Day 12: The Great Crossing
📅 Wed 24 Dec | 🛌 Stay: Golden Hotel, Christchurch  
🚗 Drive: 3.5 hrs

**09:00 AM:** Depart via Arthur's Pass.

**10:30 AM:** Otira Viaduct Lookout.

**11:30 AM:** Castle Hill (Kura Tawhiti).

> 🏰 **Activity:** Run around the giant limestone boulders (Narnia filming location).

**03:00 PM:** Arrive Christchurch. Drop bags.

**Evening:** Relax. It's Christmas Eve, many places close early.

**Dinner:** Book a table at Coriander's (St Asaph St) or ensure you buy groceries early.

---

## 📍 PART 4: North Island Geothermal & Hobbits

### Day 13: Northward Bound
📅 Thu 25 Dec (Xmas) | 🛌 Stay: Alpine Motel, Rotorua  
✈️ Flight: CHC → AKL (2:00 PM - 3:30 PM)

**Morning:** Leisurely breakfast. Drop rental car.

**Flight:** Arrive Auckland. Pick up new Rental Car.

**Drive:** Auckland to Rotorua (3 hrs).

> ⚠️ **Note:** It is Christmas Day. Almost ALL restaurants / supermarkets will be CLOSED.
>
> **Action:** You MUST book a hotel dinner or ensure your motel allows cooking and you bring food from a gas station convenience store (which stay open).
>
> 💡 **Tip:** Some Indian restaurants might open on Xmas, call Paradise Indian in Auckland or Indian Star in Rotorua NOW to check.

---

### Day 14: Mud & Redwoods
📅 Fri 26 Dec (Boxing Day) | 🛌 Stay: Alpine Motel, Rotorua

**09:30 AM:** Te Puia. See the Pōhutu Geyser and Kiwi birds.

**01:00 PM:** Lunch at Eat Streat (Restaurant hub).

**03:00 PM:** Skyline Rotorua. If you loved the Luge in QT, the tracks here are longer and arguably better.

**08:00 PM:** Redwoods Treewalk (Night Lights). Suspension bridges illuminated by lanterns. Magical.

---

### Day 15: The Ultimate Combo (Glowworms & Hobbits)
📅 Sat 27 Dec | 🛌 Stay: Auckland Accom  
🎟️ Pre-Booked: Waitomo (12 PM) | Hobbiton (5:30 PM)

**08:00 AM:** Depart Rotorua early.

**08:45 AM:** Putaruru Blue Spring. Walk: 15 mins from Leslie Road carpark to see the blue water.

**10:15 AM:** Drive to Waitomo.

**12:00 PM:** Waitomo Glowworm Caves Tour. Boat ride under the "stars". (45 mins).

**01:30 PM:** Lunch at Waitomo Cafe.

**02:30 PM:** Drive to Matamata (1.5 hrs).

**05:30 PM:** Hobbiton Movie Set Tour. Enjoy the Shire in the evening light.

**08:00 PM:** Drive to Auckland (2 hrs).

**10:00 PM:** Check-in Auckland.

---

### Day 16: City of Sails
📅 Sun 28 Dec | 🛌 Stay: Auckland Accom

**10:00 AM:** Kelly Tarlton's Sea Life Aquarium. Penguins and Shark Tunnels. Great for the 2yo.

**01:00 PM:** Lunch: Saravanaa Bhavan (Auckland CBD) - World famous Dosas.

**03:00 PM:** Sky Tower. Views of the harbour.

**05:00 PM:** Viaduct Harbour walk.

**Final Dinner:** Cassia (Modern Indian) or 1947 Eatery.

---

### Day 17: Departure
📅 Mon 29 Dec

Drive to AKL Airport. Drop car. Fly home with "mindblowing" memories! ✈️🇳🇿
